## Identity

**Stage name:** EON  
**Pronunciation:** `ee-on`  
**Permanent member ID:** `M07`

## Musical positions

**Primary:** High Vocal / Upper Harmony Specialist  
**Secondary:** Counter-melody / ad-libs / selective power belt

## Personality

Playful, detail-oriented, highly confident onstage, perfectionistic about performance without being rigid socially.

## Performance identity

**The Flare.** Appears selectively so that the top of the arrangement feels genuinely higher when he enters.

## Vocal DNA

Clear high tenor, strong upper register, controlled mixed voice, sustained upper notes, precise harmonies, agile ad-libs, and counter-melodies. Brighter and more airborne than SOREN rather than simply louder.

## Best uses

- pre-chorus ceiling lift;
- upper chorus harmonies;
- final-chorus ad-libs;
- counter-melodies;
- sustained high notes;
- selective climax belts.

## Avoid

- using him continuously from the first verse;
- making every song a high-note showcase;
- duplicating SOREN's chest-dominant power role.

## Last Train Home compatibility

The approved debut contains dedicated `[High Vocal]` roles in Pre-Chorus 2, the bridge/build, and final-chorus ad-libs. EON maps directly to those functions and preserves the song's deliberate late-arrangement vocal escalation.
