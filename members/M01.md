Observant, steady, self-controlled, dry sense of humor, naturally protective without becoming paternalistic.

**The Anchor.** Uses stillness, eye contact, low-register authority, and economical movement rather than constant intensity. He should remain convincing when the production around him becomes chaotic.

## Vocal / rap DNA

Dark resonant baritone; clean low melodic lines; deep controlled rap with spacious cadence, deliberate pauses, and strong consonant weight.

## Best uses

- openings;
- low verse anchors;
- sparse deep-rap entries;
- bridge openings;
- spoken lines;
- low octave support under group vocals.

## Avoid

- routine high-register climax lines;
- writing him with the same cadence as ROOK.
