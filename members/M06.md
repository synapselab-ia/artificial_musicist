Mid-range male voice with flexible speech-to-song phrasing, melodic rap, vulnerable cadence, controlled rhythmic singing, and credible transitions between bars and sustained melody.

## Best uses

- rap-to-pre-chorus transitions;
- melodic rap;
- emotionally exposed Verse 2 sections;
- rhythmic hooks;
- bridges that need both speech and melody.

## Avoid

- turning him into a weaker copy of both the rappers and singers;
- separating his singing and rapping so completely that the hybrid identity disappears.
