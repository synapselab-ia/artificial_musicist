# SONG WORKFLOW TEMPLATE

Use this sequence for new releases unless the user explicitly changes the process.

## Stage 1 — Concept

Provide approximately 3–5 strong concepts. Each should include:

1. provisional English title;
2. central idea;
3. story/message;
4. primary emotion;
5. musical direction;
6. possible English hook concept;
7. vocal/rap approach;
8. why it could work as a modern K-pop song.

Do not write full lyrics yet.

## Stage 2 — Sonic identity

Define only musically justified elements:

- primary/secondary genre;
- BPM;
- duration;
- mood;
- rhythmic feel;
- drums;
- bass;
- synths;
- guitars/keys/strings where relevant;
- vocal production;
- rap production;
- dynamic range.

## Stage 3 — Architecture

Design sections and energy progression before full lyrics. Use only the sections the song needs.

## Stage 4 — Hook

Propose 3–5 concise hook directions. Identify the exact phrase the listener should remember after one play.

## Stage 5 — Lyrics

Write natural English lyrics optimized for singability, rhythm, strong vowels, concise imagery, internal rhyme, coherent metaphor, vocal identity, and performance-ready rap.

## Initial generation

Prepare:

- DETAILS;
- SOUND;
- LYRICS;
- GENERATION PROMPT.

All Flow/Lyria-facing material must be in English.

## Diagnosis after generation

Always separate:

### PRESERVE
What already works.

### CHANGE
What specifically needs improvement.

### INTERVENTION LEVEL
- SMALL — localized adjustment;
- MEDIUM — meaningful change to one or more sections;
- LARGE — remix or structural reconstruction.

Prefer surgical edits when most of the track already works.

## Finalization

A song becomes official canon only after explicit user approval of a generated version. Update `docs/DISCOGRAPHY.md`, the song folder, and `docs/CHECKPOINT.md` when canon status changes.
