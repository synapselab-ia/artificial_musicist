## Locked concept

A complete statement is clipped, reposted, retold, and stripped of context until the circulating fragment means the opposite of what was said.

Original statement:

**“I don't hate where I came from / I hate being told I can't outgrow it.”**

Circulating misquote:

**“I hate where I came from.”**

Emotional engine:

`misquote -> correction -> forensic dissection -> reconstruction -> clean correction -> refusal -> ownership`
