# SIXTH RELEASE — SONG ARCHITECTURE ROUND

Status: **OPEN — AWAITING USER SELECTION**

Date: 2026-09-05

Locked concept: **DON'T QUOTE ME**  
Locked sonic direction: **A — RECEIPT TAPE**

## Architecture objective

The song must feel rap-forward because rap drives the narrative and sectional motion, not merely because one long rap verse exists.

All options below preserve:

- meaningful functions for all seven UNFRAME members;
- distinct VALE / ROOK / MIRO rap identities;
- substantial melodic jobs for SOREN / NOEN / ELIAN / EON;
- section-level Flow/Lyria-compatible vocal blocks;
- a progression from clipped / manipulated context toward one uninterrupted final statement;
- internal essential-song budget of approximately **2:45–2:50**;
- later Flow-facing duration request of approximately **2:55–2:58**, with the song finishing cleanly by approximately **2:59** and never exceeding **3:00**;
- no extra filler chorus, dance break, solo, or long intro/outro to consume the duration margin.

## A — CHAIN OF CUSTODY

Dramatic principle: **trace how one sentence gets altered at every handoff**.

Structure:

`CHOPPED COLD OPEN -> VALE / ORIGINAL STATEMENT -> ELIAN PRE-CHORUS -> CHORUS 1 / MISQUOTED VERSION -> ROOK / CHAIN-OF-CUSTODY RAP -> MIRO / RECONSTRUCTION -> NOEN / CLEAN CORRECTION -> CHORUS 2 / TAKE IT WHOLE -> SHORT BRIDGE / NO EDITS -> FINAL CHORUS / FULL CONTEXT -> EON + GROUP OUTRO TAG`

How it works:

The opening establishes one clear thought, then the first chorus deliberately presents a distorted or incomplete version of it. ROOK dissects the path from original line to reposted fragment in the song's densest technical block. MIRO begins putting the meaning back together. NOEN finally gets a stripped, uninterrupted correction with almost no production interference. The last third then stops chopping the lead vocal and allows the complete statement to land.

Seven-member map:

- VALE: original low-register statement and measured rebuttal;
- ELIAN: melodic setup into the first hook;
- SOREN: main chorus identity;
- ROOK: longest and most technical rap centerpiece;
- MIRO: reconstruction block between rap and melody;
- NOEN: exposed truth/correction block;
- EON: late counterline and final upper-register expansion.

Strength:

The narrative is exceptionally clear and the architecture itself explains the title concept.

Risk:

Because Chorus 1 intentionally carries a distorted version of the statement, hook writing must ensure the listener understands that the distortion is deliberate rather than simply receiving a confusing first chorus.

## B — CUT / PASTE

Dramatic principle: **interrupt the song itself before lines can finish**.

Structure:

`CUT-UP INTRO -> VALE RAP BLOCK -> HOOK FRAGMENT -> ROOK RAP BLOCK -> ELIAN LIFT -> CHORUS 1 -> MIRO RAP/SUNG BLOCK -> NOEN INTERRUPTION -> ROOK + VALE SECOND RAP MOVEMENT -> CHORUS 2 -> BRIDGE / LET ME FINISH -> FINAL CHORUS / UNCUT -> EON FINAL LINE`

How it works:

The first half behaves almost like an argument with an editor: sections are short, abruptly muted, restarted, and recontextualized. Rap therefore occupies a large proportion of the song without requiring every rapper to share one continuous verse. The bridge is the first explicit structural demand for uninterrupted space — after that point, the final chorus is the longest continuous melodic section in the track.

Seven-member map:

- VALE: first major rap block and return in the second rap movement;
- ROOK: technical second block plus late rhythmic escalation;
- MIRO: hybrid midpoint that keeps rap central while widening melody;
- ELIAN: melodic lift into Chorus 1;
- SOREN: stable chorus lead;
- NOEN: intentionally isolated interruption / emotional hinge;
- EON: final high-line completion rather than constant ad-lib presence.

Strength:

Most obviously **rap-forward** in structural proportion while still preserving a proper K-pop chorus.

Risk:

Many short section changes can tempt Flow to blur intended boundaries or compress lyrics, so the eventual lyric and prompt must stay especially economical.

## C — ON THE RECORD

Dramatic principle: **start defensive, then stop explaining**.

Structure:

`RECEIPT-TAPE INTRO -> VALE / ON THE RECORD -> ELIAN PRE-CHORUS -> CHORUS 1 -> ROOK TECHNICAL RAP -> MIRO COUNTER-RAP -> CHORUS 2 -> NOEN BRIDGE / I SAID WHAT I SAID -> VALE SHORT RETURN -> FINAL CHORUS / QUOTE THE WHOLE THING -> EON + GROUP FINAL STACK`

How it works:

This is the cleanest architecture. The first half follows a strong rap/vocal alternation, with ROOK and MIRO receiving separate consecutive rap identities rather than one generic shared rap sequence. NOEN's bridge changes the narrator's posture from correcting everyone to refusing further defensive explanation. The final chorus then turns `don't quote me` into ownership rather than avoidance.

Seven-member map:

- VALE: low opening and brief final rap punctuation;
- ELIAN: first melodic rise;
- SOREN: recurring chorus lead;
- ROOK: technical rap centerpiece;
- MIRO: separate melodic-rhythmic counter-rap;
- NOEN: exposed bridge state change;
- EON: final upper stack and counterline.

Strength:

Lowest Flow-rendering risk and easiest architecture for strong diction, complete lyric delivery, and a clean sub-3:00 ending.

Risk:

More conventional section ordering means the lyric and production details must work harder to make the quotation concept feel unique.

## D — UNEDITED

Dramatic principle: **the whole song changes state halfway through**.

Structure:

`CORRUPTED INTRO -> VALE RAP -> ROOK RAP -> CLIPPED CHORUS 1 -> MIRO REASSEMBLY -> ELIAN LIFT -> FULL CHORUS 2 -> NOEN BRIDGE / SILENCE -> VALE + ROOK SHORT FINAL RAP -> FINAL CHORUS / UNEDITED -> EON / COMPLETE LAST LINE`

How it works:

The first half is deliberately fragmented and rap-dominant: VALE and ROOK arrive before the song permits a complete chorus. MIRO becomes the transition mechanism that starts reconnecting the musical material. From that point onward, section lengths become more stable, harmony widens, interruptions disappear, and the song behaves increasingly like one continuous statement. The final chorus is not just bigger — it is structurally **uncut**.

Seven-member map:

- VALE: first grounded rap and late return;
- ROOK: second, more technical rap and late rhythmic callback;
- MIRO: exact midpoint reconstruction mechanism;
- ELIAN: first fully melodic rise after the fragmented half;
- SOREN: clipped first chorus evolving into complete later chorus states;
- NOEN: silence-heavy bridge before the final confrontation;
- EON: delivers the highest complete final line after all fragmentation stops.

Strength:

Strongest macro-level transformation and probably the most dramatic use of `RECEIPT TAPE` as a production concept.

Risk:

Delaying the first fully satisfying chorus can reduce immediate hook payoff if the opening rap writing is not exceptionally strong.

## Selection lens

- **A — CHAIN OF CUSTODY**: clearest storytelling and strongest concept mechanics;
- **B — CUT / PASTE**: highest rap proportion and most aggressive sectional movement;
- **C — ON THE RECORD**: safest Flow execution and cleanest pop/rap balance;
- **D — UNEDITED**: strongest whole-song transformation from fragmentation to continuity.
