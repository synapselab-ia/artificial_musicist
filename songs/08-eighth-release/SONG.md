## Locked concept

One member admits that something in his life is going badly. Before he can finish explaining, the other six immediately begin offering solutions.

The advice conflicts: leave, stay, confront, wait, be rational, stop overthinking. The more everyone tries to help, the less room remains for the person who actually has the problem.

The central turn is:

**“Can one of you ask me what I want?”**

The song is not anti-advice. It distinguishes genuine help from replacing somebody else's agency with one's own preferred solution.

## Mandatory dramatic property — dialogue

This song must feel like a real discussion among the seven UNFRAME members.

Use **coherent conversational blocks**, not fragile one-line synthetic identity switching:

- a statement must receive an actual response;
- rebuttals should reference what was just said;
- positions may interrupt, complicate, or reverse one another;
- later lines may expose that apparently helpful advice is becoming pressure;
- the bridge must create the first true listening space;
- the final section must demonstrate changed behavior, not merely repeat the moral.

## Emotional / dialogue engine

`confession -> unsolicited solution -> competing solution -> advice pile-up -> speaker disappears -> frustration -> silence -> request for agency -> actual listening`

## Locked sonic direction — B: TALKBACK

**Rap-forward boom-bap / contemporary hip-hop + syncopated funk bass + stop-start K-pop arrangement.**

Working tempo: approximately **112–116 BPM**.

Core production behavior:

- punchy acoustic/electronic hybrid drums;
- tight syncopated bass riff;
- clipped brass, organ, or percussive chord stabs used like verbal punctuation;
- beat dropouts after key rebuttals;
- brief group reactions such as breaths, “wait,” “what?”, and half-spoken fragments used as rhythmic texture rather than extra full lines;
- different advice blocks may occupy different pockets without changing the entire instrumental identity;
- maximum verbal density immediately before the bridge;
- near-unaccompanied bridge question;
- final section preserves groove but removes the verbal pile-on and leaves audible room after NOEN's lines.
