## Locked concept requirements

`NO ONE ASKED` is a seven-member discussion, not a monologue with feature verses.

Mandatory behavior:

- one position must provoke an audible response from another;
- members should receive coherent blocks long enough for Flow to establish contrasting delivery;
- the arrangement may use brief overlaps, interruptions, backing reactions, and echoed fragments, but must not depend on seven fragile one-line voice switches;
- the advice pile-up must become increasingly overwhelming;
- the bridge question **“Can one of you ask me what I want?”** must create a clear state change through sudden space;
- the final section must sound like the group has learned to listen, not merely become quieter.

### Locked dialogue behavior

This is the most openly argumentative direction.

Each major advice block gets a different pocket without changing the entire song:

- VALE can sit behind the beat with a blunt, practical answer;
- ROOK pushes forward with precise internal rhyme and a harder counterargument;
- MIRO destabilizes both sides by switching between rap and melodic speech;
- ELIAN and SOREN gradually turn supportive advice into melodic pressure;
- EON adds upper responses / reactions without becoming generic decoration;
- NOEN remains the person whose original problem is progressively buried until the bridge rupture.

The first chorus should behave less like a conventional huge release and more like a structured pile-up of answers.

At maximum verbal density, the song cuts to nearly unaccompanied NOEN for:

**“Can one of you ask me what I want?”**

The final state keeps the groove but removes the verbal pile-on. Replies become shorter and leave audible room after NOEN's lines.
