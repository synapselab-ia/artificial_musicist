## Flow-compatible dialogue rule

UNFRAME still has seven canonical members, but the generation should not rely on seven fragile one-line synthetic identities.

The intended solution is **dialogue by coherent blocks**:

- one member or faction states a position;
- another member or faction receives enough consecutive space to rebut it;
- a third voice complicates or reframes the disagreement;
- melodic members can turn the argument into a shared refrain, emotional consequence, or temporary consensus;
- later sections may reverse which side appears convincing;
- all seven members must have a meaningful function across the discussion, but not necessarily seven equal solo blocks.

Canonical conversational strengths:

- `M01 — VALE`: low-authority opening statement / grounded position;
- `M04 — ROOK`: precise technical rebuttal / evidence / escalation;
- `M06 — MIRO`: flexible counterpoint that can move between speech, rap, and melody;
- `M03 — NOEN`: quiet personal consequence or the line that exposes what the argument is actually about;
- `M05 — ELIAN`: connective melodic response / reframing;
- `M02 — SOREN`: major emotional claim or chorus-level position;
- `M07 — EON`: upper-register counterline, late contradiction, or final expansion.

The eventual song should feel like people are listening and reacting to what came immediately before them.

## Selected concept — E: NO ONE ASKED

One member finally admits that something in his life is going badly.

Before he can finish explaining, the other six start fixing it.

One says leave. One says stay. One says confront them. One says wait. One says be rational. One says stop overthinking. Every piece of advice contradicts another, and the person who actually has the problem gradually disappears beneath everybody else's certainty about what he should do.

The bridge finally stops the pile-on:

**"Can one of you ask me what I want?"**

The song is not anti-advice. It is about the difference between helping somebody and replacing their agency with your solution.

Dialogue engine:

`confession -> advice 1 -> rebuttal/advice 2 -> six-way solution pile-up -> frustration -> silence -> "ask me" -> actual listening`

Seven-member use:

- six members can occupy three or four coherent advice blocs rather than six one-line cameos;
- VALE and ROOK can provide opposing hard solutions;
- MIRO can keep interrupting the binary with hybrid/conditional answers;
- ELIAN and SOREN can make apparently supportive advice sound increasingly overwhelming as the arrangement grows;
- NOEN is especially suited to the person whose quiet original problem keeps getting buried;
- EON can transform earlier advice fragments into softer listening responses in the final section.

Primary freshness guard: this is about **unsolicited solutions versus agency and listening**, not being everybody's emergency contact or transactional kindness.
