## Draft intent

The lyric uses a concrete work/burnout scenario so the disagreement has real stakes instead of remaining abstract. The advice is intentionally plausible: nobody is written as stupid or malicious. The failure is that the group mistakes NOEN's uncertainty for a request to decide for him.

The first chorus locks the selected title nucleus **“No one asked, everybody answered.”** The second chorus compresses earlier advice into controlled commands and reaches maximum pressure. The bridge then creates the mandated state change, and EON is the first member to perform the new behavior by simply asking **“What do you want?”**

The final chorus mutates the hook into **“Now you ask, I can answer”** and resolves the earlier `sentence` image with **“for once, I get to finish the sentence.”**

## Duration guard

The draft is intentionally tighter than a maximal lyric. Do not add filler choruses or extra advice blocks merely because Flow may allow them. Final timing must be validated after lyric approval and prompt construction under the project's two-budget method.
