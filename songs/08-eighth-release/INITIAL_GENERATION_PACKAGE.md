## First-generation diagnosis priorities

When audio returns, check in this order:

1. material lyric omissions or substitutions;
2. whether the dialogue reads as responses rather than unrelated verses;
3. whether the original speaker is audibly distinct from the advising room;
4. whether the brief interruption cluster stays intelligible without destructive micro-switching;
5. whether VALE-function low rap, ROOK-function technical rap, and MIRO-function melodic rap feel different;
6. whether `No.` creates a hard reset;
7. whether the bridge is exposed and the requested silence is real;
8. whether `What do you want?` arrives from a clearly different voice after silence;
9. whether the final chorus changes behavior from commands to listening;
10. musical-content endpoint relative to the 3:00 hard ceiling;
11. robotic-vocal artifacts, especially in dense rap or group responses.

Do not reject solely for minor micro-line attribution drift if the dramatic conversation remains intact.
