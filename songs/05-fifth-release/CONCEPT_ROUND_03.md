## Selected concept

### C — LEAVE THE LIGHT ON

Someone leaves after a rupture. The narrator refuses to chase them, beg, or promise that everything can be fixed.

But they leave one light on anyway.

Emotional engine:

`rupture -> pride -> silence -> waiting -> vulnerability -> unresolved invitation`

Why it was selected:

- one simple physical image can carry the entire song;
- dignity and hope can coexist without forcing reconciliation;
- the premise is emotionally legible before any production or lyrical machinery is added;
- it supports UNFRAME's restraint-versus-release identity while still allowing a different sonic world from the approved catalogue.
