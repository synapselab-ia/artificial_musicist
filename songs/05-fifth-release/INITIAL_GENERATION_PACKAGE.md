## Exact single-prompt handoff

```text
Create a complete original song titled “LEAVE THE LIGHT ON” for an original seven-member male K-pop group. Use alternative R&B blended with UK garage / 2-step and atmospheric modern K-pop, around 118–122 BPM. The emotional core is restraint versus unresolved hope: someone has left after a rupture; the narrator refuses to chase, beg, call repeatedly, or promise that everything can be fixed, but deliberately leaves one light on. The song must preserve dignity and agency throughout and must not become a generic plea for reconciliation.

Keep the production intimate but moving: soft restless 2-step drums, warm sub-bass, clipped guitar or pluck details, airy keys, close dry verses with rhythmic negative space, and choruses that widen harmonically without turning into a giant EDM drop. Subtle reversed vocal tails or atmospheric vocal textures are fine, but do not let them become a gimmick. The narrator may be waiting emotionally, but the track itself should keep moving.

Use coherent section-level male vocal blocks rather than frequent line-by-line singer switching. Use a low resonant baritone for the Cold Open, Verse 2, and Outro; an intimate conversational tenor for Verse 1 and the exposed Bridge; a bright clean tenor for the Pre-Chorus; a full-bodied power tenor for the choruses; one compact technical rapper for the complete rap block with crisp articulation and pocket rather than excessive speed; a melodic rap / vocal hybrid for the transition after the rap; and a clear high tenor mainly as late upper harmony in the Final Chorus. ## Evaluation priorities after generation
```

2. whether the rap remains articulated without sounding rushed;
3. whether the UK-garage / 2-step motion feels wistful rather than celebratory;
4. whether Chorus 1 is memorable without becoming oversized;
5. whether the Bridge actually strips/exposes the state change;
6. whether the Final Chorus clearly mutates `I won't wait up` into `I didn't wait up`;
7. whether the Outro `No message / No footsteps / Still on` is complete;
8. whether the song lands near the requested duration without clipping or filler.
