# LEAVE THE LIGHT ON — SONIC DIRECTION ROUND

Status: **OPEN — USER SELECTION REQUIRED**

Date: 2026-09-04

## Design target

The song must preserve the selected concept's emotional tension — dignity versus hope — without becoming a second `Last Train Home`.

The approved catalogue already contains cinematic R&B-pop, industrial electro-pop, neo-soul/minimal funk, and dark art-pop/electro-R&B. This round therefore opens new but still credible UNFRAME territory.

No architecture, hook, lyric, exact line distribution, or generation prompt is locked yet.

---

## A — PORCHLIGHT PULSE

**Alternative R&B + UK garage / 2-step + atmospheric K-pop**

Working tempo: approximately **118–122 BPM**.

Core sound:

- soft but restless 2-step drums;
- warm sub-bass rather than aggressive low end;
- clipped guitar/pluck details and airy keys;
- close, dry verses with rhythmic negative space;
- chorus widens harmonically without becoming a giant EDM drop;
- subtle vocal chops or reversed tails used as atmosphere, not as a gimmick;
- final section can become more luminous while the lyric remains unresolved.

Emotional behavior:

The narrator is waiting, but the track itself is still moving. That prevents the concept from collapsing into static sadness.

UNFRAME fit:

- NOEN can carry intimate close-mic material;
- ELIAN naturally lifts transitions;
- MIRO can bridge sung and rhythmic phrasing;
- ROOK can use a compact, articulate rap pocket without forcing speed;
- SOREN can widen the chorus;
- EON can provide late upper shimmer rather than an obligatory huge high note;
- VALE can anchor the opening or a low post-rupture section.

Primary advantage:

**Best balance of intimacy, modern K-pop movement, and catalogue differentiation.**

Primary risk:

If overproduced, the garage rhythm could make the song feel too buoyant for the lyric. The groove must remain wistful rather than celebratory.

## B — HOUSE STILL WARM

**Contemporary pop-rock + R&B undertone + modern K-pop vocal production**

Working tempo: approximately **100–104 BPM**.

Core sound:

- muted electric-guitar figures in the verses;
- real-feeling bass and live-drum weight;
- restrained synth bed underneath rather than dominating the arrangement;
- chorus expands into full-band impact;
- bridge can strip to guitar/voice before rebuilding;
- final section should feel physically larger, not electronically denser.

Emotional behavior:

The song sounds like a place that still carries someone's presence after they leave: warm, physical, lived-in, but increasingly empty.

UNFRAME fit:

- strong room for SOREN as a real power-vocal payoff;
- ELIAN can carry melodic rise without spending SOREN too early;
- VALE gains useful contrast against organic instrumentation;
- ROOK and MIRO can inhabit a half-time or syncopated middle block;
- EON can create live-feeling upper harmonies in the final lift.

Primary advantage:

**Would give UNFRAME its clearest band-driven release so far.**

Primary risk:

If written too broadly, it can drift into generic pop-rock heartbreak. The production and lyric need specific detail and restraint.

## C — AMBER STATIC

**Warm synth-pop + breakbeat + dream-pop texture**

Working tempo: approximately **112–116 BPM**.

Core sound:

- glowing analog-style pads;
- dry breakbeat drums with occasional gated accents;
- rounded bass movement;
- small bell-like or glassy synth motif representing the light;
- verses feel slightly suspended, choruses more open and radiant;
- no retro cosplay and no overt synthwave clichés;
- final chorus can become brighter without resolving harmonically into triumph.

Emotional behavior:

The light itself becomes almost audible: one small warm signal inside a darker, electrically textured space.

UNFRAME fit:

- strong contrast between VALE/NOEN intimacy and SOREN/EON expansion;
- MIRO can sit naturally between rhythmic verse phrasing and melody;
- ROOK can occupy a textured breakbeat pocket;
- layered group vocals can bloom gradually rather than arrive all at once.

Primary advantage:

**Most visually coherent route with the title image while remaining musically distinct from the existing catalogue.**

Primary risk:

Too much atmosphere could make the song emotionally pretty but passive. The drums and vocal phrasing must maintain forward tension.

---

## D — FRONT STEP

**Modern soul + acoustic/electric guitar + restrained hip-hop/R&B**

Working tempo: approximately **92–96 BPM**.

Core sound:

- close guitar figure as the main harmonic object;
- sub-bass entering selectively rather than continuously;
- rim clicks, soft kick, sparse percussion;
- occasional organ or warm keyboard support;
- little production ornament in the opening half;
- vocal stacks become the main source of scale in the final section.

Emotional behavior:

This is the most exposed route. It treats leaving the light on as an almost embarrassingly small private gesture rather than a cinematic symbol.

UNFRAME fit:

- NOEN and VALE gain unusually exposed space;
- MIRO can deliver conversational melodic rap;
- ROOK must favor pocket and precision over speed;
- SOREN's eventual entry can feel enormous precisely because the arrangement has held back;
- EON can complete the final vocal stack instead of dominating it.

Primary advantage:

**Could produce the most emotionally naked version of the concept.**

Primary risk:

It sits closest to territory already explored by `THE QUIET PART` in terms of restraint and lyric-first pacing, even though the instrumentation would be different.

---

## Recommendation

**A — PORCHLIGHT PULSE** is the strongest default route.

It preserves the intimacy required by the concept but gives the song rhythmic motion, a modern male K-pop frame, and a sonic identity not yet represented in the approved UNFRAME catalogue.
