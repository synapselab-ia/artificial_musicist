## Selected architecture — A: THE LADDER

Dramatic principle: **each section climbs one more rung until the narrator realizes the ladder is leaning against nothing he actually wants**.

Canonical section map:

`COLD SCORE -> RAP VERSE 1 / COUNTING UP -> PRE-HOOK -> CHORUS 1 / AHEAD -> RAP VERSE 2 / MORE CATEGORIES -> ROOK TECHNICAL ASCENT -> MIRO POCKET BREAK -> CHORUS 2 / STILL NOT ENOUGH -> BRIDGE / WHAT AM I WINNING? -> FINAL CHORUS / OFF THE BOARD -> GROUP TAG`

Canonical behavior:

- opening begins with literal score/count punctuation and a low, controlled VALE entrance;
- first rap establishes the addictive pleasure of being ahead;
- Chorus 1 is short and catchy, celebrating the lead without yet questioning it;
- second half expands the scoreboard from money/status into praise, beauty, opportunities, followers, rooms, names, and friendship;
- ROOK receives the largest technical rap block, with selective double-time escalation;
- MIRO interrupts with a slower, elastic pocket that starts exposing the absurdity of the counting;
- Chorus 2 sounds bigger but reveals that every win immediately creates another category to lose;
- Bridge strips most percussion and asks the irreversible question: **what do I actually want when nobody else is below me?**;
- final chorus abandons the ranking premise rather than merely declaring first place;
- production removes more scoreboard punctuation as the song ends.

Seven-member function sketch:

- VALE: cold open / low punctuation;
- ROOK: technical rap centerpiece;
- MIRO: pocket-switch rap and transition;
- NOEN: exposed bridge realization;
- ELIAN: pre-hook melodic lift;
- SOREN: main chorus lead;
- EON: final upper harmony / group tag.
