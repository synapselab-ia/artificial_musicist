## Selected concept — C: ZERO SUM

A person or social environment turns everything into a scoreboard: attention, money, beauty, status, opportunities, followers, praise, even friendship. If someone else wins, they experience it as a personal loss.

The narrator initially gets pulled into the ranking system and starts measuring himself the same way. The turn comes when he realizes that needing everyone else to become smaller is not winning at all.

Emotional engine:

`comparison -> competition -> temporary high -> escalation -> disgust -> refusal of the scoreboard -> self-defined win`

Why it fits a rap-forward UNFRAME track:

- rap can move through different scorekeeping systems with rhythmic escalation;
- ROOK and MIRO can embody different relationships to competition rather than delivering one isolated showcase;
- melodic sections can weaponize the addictive rush of being ahead before the bridge exposes the emptiness;
- all seven functions can participate without requiring seven micro-solo identities.

Primary freshness guard: this is about **manufactured scarcity and competitive comparison**, not perfectionism or identity performance.
