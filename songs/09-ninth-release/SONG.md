## Locked concept

After a difficult stretch, the narrator has become accustomed to living closed off: curtains shut, messages ignored, room stale, days repeating.

There is no dramatic recovery scene.

One morning, he opens the window.

At first, the outside world is almost irritating: traffic, neighbors, somebody laughing, wind moving things around. But those sounds also prove that life kept going while he was hiding from it.

He is not suddenly healed. He is simply willing to let some of the world back in.

Core line of thought:

**“I don't feel better yet, but I opened the window.”**

## Emotional engine

`withdrawal -> stagnant routine -> small physical action -> sensory return -> discomfort -> cautious participation`
