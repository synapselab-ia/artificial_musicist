## Draft intent

The lyric stays concrete and domestic rather than naming recovery in abstract terms. Chorus 1 only shows physical evidence that the sealed room has changed. Chorus 2 reveals **“I opened the window”** as an action, not a breakthrough. The bridge explicitly protects the incomplete-recovery premise. The final chorus mutates the action into the choice **“I left it open anyway.”**

The rhythmic middle-eight is the only substantial rap/rhythmic section. It inventories the closed-room habits in a concise, image-driven way and then hands the song back to melody permanently.

## Duration guard

This draft intentionally uses fewer words and more melodic space than the recent rap-forward songs. Protect chorus phrasing, harmonies, live-band transitions, and the bridge pause rather than filling every available second with text.
