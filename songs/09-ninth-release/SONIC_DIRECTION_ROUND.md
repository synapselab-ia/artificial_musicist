## Locked concept

**OPEN WINDOW**

Core thought:

**“I don't feel better yet, but I opened the window.”**

Mandatory user direction:

- vocal-first / melodic-first;
- rap is secondary rather than structural engine;
- meaning must not depend on seven individually traceable synthetic voices;
- preserve UNFRAME through vocal color, harmony, register contrast, strong hook construction, bridge state change, and transformed final section;
- avoid a generic healing ballad.

## Selected sonic direction — B: CURTAINS MOVING

**Indie-pop / alternative rock + clean live-band dynamics + modern K-pop vocal production.**

Working tempo: approximately **112–118 BPM**.

### Core sound

- live-feeling drums with light room sound;
- warm electric bass;
- clean guitar picking in verses;
- brighter strummed / chiming guitars as the song opens;
- restrained synth support only for width and upper atmosphere;
- final chorus grows through natural band expansion rather than maximalist production.

### Vocal behavior

- NOEN: intimate opening / closed-room center;
- ELIAN: pre-chorus lift and melodic connector;
- SOREN: broad chorus payoff without power-ballad excess;
- EON: upper counterline / increasing air and brightness;
- VALE: low sung or rhythmic grounding rather than a full rap verse;
- ROOK + MIRO: one concise rhythmic middle-eight / contrast block, closer to spoken cadence and melodic rhythm than dense technical rap.

### Narrative-production arc

The first section should feel physically sealed: close voice, restrained drums, narrow guitar field. As the window opens, the band progressively becomes more present and breathable. The bridge may briefly reduce to voice plus a minimal guitar/bass pulse before the final chorus enters with the fullest natural-band sound.

### User intent preserved

This is intentionally a major catalog departure from the recent rap-forward run. Rap remains secondary and should occupy a clear minority of the finished song. Vocal melody, harmony, band dynamics, and chorus lift are the primary structural engine.

### Risk guard

Avoid generic inspirational pop-rock. The lyric must remain concrete, sensory, understated, and unresolved enough to preserve the concept's central truth: opening the window is a small act of re-entry, not proof that everything is fixed.
