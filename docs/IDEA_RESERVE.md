### DRESS REHEARSAL

Stored from: F6 new-song concept round, 2026-09-04.
User disposition: **liked / preserve for possible future use**.
Current status: **reserve — not selected for release two**.

Core concept: people keep living as if the real version of their life starts later—after they are ready, after they stop making mistakes, after they become who others expect. The song turns when they stop rehearsing for an imaginary future and choose the imperfect present instead.

Core emotional engine:

`self-control -> hesitation -> recognition -> deliberate risk -> release`

Why preserve it:

- strong theatrical metaphor without needing to be about fame;
- natural dynamic escalation;
- strong fit with UNFRAME's identity of rejecting fixed frames;
- substantial roles for all seven members;
- viable future route for an anthemic but non-generic self-liberation song.

### NO SAINTS AFTER MIDNIGHT

Stored from: F6 new-song concept round, 2026-09-04.
User disposition: **liked / preserve for possible future use**.
Current status: **reserve — not selected for release two**.

Core concept: during the day, people are polished, responsible, diplomatic, and carefully edited. After midnight, the masks come off—not as a crime or debauchery fantasy, but as permission to be messy, honest, impulsive, loud, flirtatious, contradictory, and fully present for a few hours. The real target is the exhaustion of perfection, not a generic bad-boy persona.

Core emotional engine:

`polished restraint -> invitation -> mask slipping -> collective release -> sunrise consequence`

Why preserve it:

- immediate title and performance identity;
- strong restraint-versus-release contrast;
- high replay and stage potential;
- gives rap and vocal members clear performance functions;
- viable future route for a confident, performance-forward UNFRAME release.
