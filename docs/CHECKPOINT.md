## F11 — ZERO SUM

Status: **IN PROGRESS — CONCEPT + SONIC DIRECTION + ARCHITECTURE LOCKED / HOOK SELECTION REQUIRED**.

### Concept

A social environment turns attention, money, status, opportunities, praise, beauty, numbers, and even friendship into a scoreboard. The narrator is pulled into the comparison system, experiences the rush of being ahead, then realizes that needing everyone else to become smaller is not a meaningful victory.

Emotional engine:

`comparison -> competition -> temporary high -> escalation -> disgust -> refusal of the scoreboard -> self-defined win`

### Sonic direction — SCOREBOARD TEETH

Minimalist hard hip-hop + modern trap + abrasive K-pop vocal architecture at approximately 98–102 BPM. Use selective double-time, dry drums, deep controlled sub, one hostile repeating motif, and small score/count punctuation while keeping main rap blocks sparse enough for diction. The bridge strips almost all percussion; the final state removes mechanical score punctuation.
