### User verdict

The user explicitly said the idea did not pass and requested moving directly to the next song rather than trying to repair it.

Observed renderer failure:

- Flow did not reliably understand where one conversational voice ended and another began;
- the user perceived approximately **four distinct voices at most** despite the seven-member design;
- the intended dialogue therefore did not read convincingly;
- since distinct conversational handoffs were structural to the concept, the defect was material.

### Permanent lesson

Do **not** design future Flow songs whose meaning depends on reliably tracking seven synthetic solo identities or rapid member-to-member voice changes.

UNFRAME remains seven-member, but full-song generation should express that through:

- stable major section roles;
- rap/vocal contrast;
- register and delivery contrast;
- lead versus group relationships;
- harmonies, counterlines, ad-libs, and ensemble presence.

Call-and-response remains allowed only when the song still makes sense if Flow collapses the interaction into a smaller number of perceptually distinct voices.

Do not enter a remix/edit loop for `NO ONE ASKED` unless the user explicitly revives it later.
