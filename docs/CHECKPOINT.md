**ZERO SUM** was selected by the user from the fresh F11 concept round on 2026-09-05.

Core premise: a person or social environment turns attention, money, status, opportunities, praise, beauty, numbers, and even friendship into a scoreboard. The narrator initially participates in the comparison system, experiences the rush of being ahead, then realizes that needing everyone else to become smaller is not a meaningful victory.

`comparison -> competition -> temporary high -> escalation -> disgust -> refusal of the scoreboard -> self-defined win`
