# GROUP CANON

Status: **FOUNDATION / PRE-NAME**

This file contains facts that must not be silently overwritten.

## Locked facts

- The project will center on one original fictional **seven-member male K-pop group**.
- Permanent member identifiers are `M01`, `M02`, `M03`, `M04`, `M05`, `M06`, and `M07`.
- The same seven fictional people should recur across songs, eras, artwork, and visual projects once their profiles are approved.
- **Last Train Home** is the official approved debut single and founding quality benchmark.
- Google Flow Music / Lyria is the primary music-generation environment at project start.
- The group must be capable of meaningful genre changes between releases.
- Group continuity comes from member identity, vocal/rap character, songwriting standards, line distribution, performance personality, and visual continuity—not from repeating one instrumental style.

## Not yet canonical

The following must not be invented and treated as final without approval:

- official group name;
- fandom name;
- logo;
- group colors;
- stage names;
- ages;
- nationalities/backgrounds;
- heights;
- exact member positions;
- faces and visual reference sheets;
- lore;
- album/era names after the debut.

## Quality benchmark

Future releases should be evaluated against the approved debut for:

- memorability;
- songwriting coherence;
- vocal identity and contrast;
- rap functionality;
- dynamic progression;
- chorus payoff;
- bridge purpose;
- final-section escalation;
- production ambition;
- replay value;
- originality.

The benchmark is a quality bar, not an instruction to recreate the debut's genre.
