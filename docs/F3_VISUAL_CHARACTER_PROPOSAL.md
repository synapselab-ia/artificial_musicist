# F3 — VISUAL CHARACTER PROPOSAL

Status: **AWAITING USER APPROVAL — NON-CANONICAL**

This proposal defines a coherent visual architecture for the seven canonical UNFRAME members. Nothing in this file becomes canonical until explicit user approval. Exact ages, nationalities/backgrounds, and numeric heights remain undefined.

## Design objective

UNFRAME must read as the same seven fictional people across songs and eras. The group should be recognizable from faces, proportions, and presence even when hair, makeup, wardrobe, lighting, and concept change completely.

The seven members are intentionally separated by face silhouette, eye shape, brow structure, jaw geometry, mouth shape, body build, and stage presence. The goal is not seven variations of the same conventionally handsome idol face.

## Global visual rules

- All seven are original fictional young adult male K-pop performers and must not be based on or resemble real celebrities.
- Photorealism should include natural skin texture, subtle facial asymmetry, believable pores, and realistic lighting rather than plastic or hyper-airbrushed skin.
- Master-reference images should use simple styling and neutral backgrounds so facial identity is easier to preserve.
- Baseline master-reference hair should be dark and natural-looking; hair color/style is **not** an immutable identity trait.
- Group images must preserve strong silhouette and facial contrast between members.
- Do not use hairstyle alone to distinguish members.
- No permanent tattoos, scars, piercings, or dramatic marks are proposed at F3 unless later explicitly approved.

## Immutable vs flexible traits

### Immutable once approved

- facial silhouette and bone structure;
- eye shape, spacing, and general placement;
- brow shape and density;
- nose proportions;
- lip and mouth proportions;
- jaw/chin proportions;
- complexion range;
- body-build proportions;
- subtle stable identifying details explicitly approved for that member.

### Era-flexible

- hair color, cut, length, texture, styling;
- makeup intensity and finish;
- wardrobe;
- accessories;
- jewelry;
- photographic lighting;
- color palette;
- location/set;
- concept-specific styling;
- facial hair, if an era deliberately uses it and recognizability is preserved.

# M01 — VALE

**Performance identity:** Anchor

### Proposed immutable visual identity

- Long rectangular-oval face with a defined but not excessively sharp jaw.
- Thick, mostly straight brows with a low, calm placement.
- Deep-set almond eyes with mildly hooded upper lids; steady rather than wide-eyed expression.
- Straight nose with a slightly broader bridge than the rest of the lineup.
- Medium lips with a relatively straight resting mouth line.
- Medium-neutral complexion with natural variation rather than porcelain finish.
- Lean frame with broader shoulders and long proportions; reads physically grounded rather than bulky.
- Stable identifying detail: very small natural beauty mark near the left jawline.

### Visual presence

Quiet authority. VALE should look strongest when doing very little: direct eye contact, relaxed posture, clean silhouette. Avoid making him overtly intimidating or hyper-masculine.

### Baseline master-reference styling

Dark near-black hair, medium-short length, controlled natural texture, subtle side separation. Minimal makeup. Charcoal crew-neck or simple structured black layer.

### Last Train Home compatibility

VALE suits the platform-night atmosphere through restrained tailoring, dark wool outerwear, reflective highlights, and still compositions near signage or windows.

# M02 — SOREN

**Performance identity:** Ignition

### Proposed immutable visual identity

- Broad oval-to-soft-square face with strong cheek structure and a solid jaw.
- Dense brows with a slight natural angle.
- Deep expressive almond eyes with subtly lowered outer corners, giving intensity without looking severe.
- Straight, substantial nose with balanced width.
- Defined cupid's bow and a visibly fuller lower lip.
- Light-medium warm-neutral complexion.
- Athletic upper-body proportions with a strong chest/shoulder line; powerful rather than bodybuilder-like.

### Visual presence

Emotional force. SOREN should look capable of moving from warmth to intensity very quickly. His face should carry large chorus moments without relying on exaggerated expressions.

### Baseline master-reference styling

Dark brown-black hair with soft volume and forehead partially visible. Clean skin, subtle eye definition. Simple black or deep navy fitted top.

### Last Train Home compatibility

SOREN works best in the debut as the member who visually opens up during larger chorus moments: stronger front light, coat movement, more direct emotional camera contact.

---

# M03 — NOEN

**Performance identity:** Lens

### Proposed immutable visual identity

- Soft oval face with a fine jaw and gently rounded cheek transition.
- Straight, medium-light brows with softer edges than VALE or ROOK.
- Slightly wider-spaced almond eyes with visible lower-lid softness; observant, intimate gaze.
- Smaller straight nose with a refined tip.
- Fuller soft-edged lips.
- Light neutral complexion with visible realistic texture.
- Slender frame with narrow shoulders and balanced, graceful proportions.
- Stable identifying detail: tiny beauty mark just below the outer area of the right eye.

### Visual presence

Intimacy without fragility. NOEN should photograph especially well in close-ups, reflections, soft window light, and emotionally quiet frames.

### Baseline master-reference styling

Natural dark hair with slightly longer fringe and soft movement. Almost invisible makeup. Fine-gauge dark knit or simple collarless layer.

### Last Train Home compatibility

NOEN is especially compatible with the opening/outro language of the debut: train-window reflections, ticket close-ups, cold platform light, and restrained eye-line acting.

# M04 — ROOK

**Performance identity:** Edge

### Proposed immutable visual identity

- Angular diamond-shaped face with high cheekbones and a narrower lower face.
- Defined brows with a sharper natural arch than any other member.
- Narrow almond eyes with a clean, focused gaze.
- Narrow straight nose with a crisp bridge.
- Thin-to-medium lips with a slightly asymmetric resting expression.
- Medium neutral-to-cool complexion.
- Compact lean-athletic build; fast, precise body language rather than broad physical presence.

### Visual presence

Precision and kinetic tension. ROOK should look visually sharp in still images and become even more distinctive in movement. Avoid turning him into a generic "bad boy" archetype.

### Baseline master-reference styling

Shorter dark textured hair with controlled irregularity, more forehead visible than NOEN. Minimal matte styling. Structured black tee or sharply cut dark jacket.

### Last Train Home compatibility

ROOK should contrast the softer vocal imagery through harder station geometry, harsher fluorescent light, clean profile shots, and fast movement during the countdown/rap section.

# M05 — ELIAN

**Performance identity:** Lift

### Proposed immutable visual identity

- Balanced heart-oval face with a clean jaw and slightly wider upper face.
- Straight medium-density brows with an open expression.
- Bright almond eyes with subtly lifted outer corners.
- Straight proportional nose, neither narrowest nor broadest in the group.
- Medium-full lips with a naturally expressive smile.
- Light-medium warm complexion.
- Lean balanced build with open posture and smooth proportions.
- Stable identifying detail: subtle right-side dimple visible only when smiling.

### Visual presence

Clarity and forward motion. ELIAN should feel visually accessible and expressive without becoming the group's permanently "cute" member.

### Baseline master-reference styling

Dark brown hair with clean soft layers and controlled volume. Natural satin skin. Deep charcoal or muted navy shirt/jacket combination.

### Last Train Home compatibility

ELIAN visually supports rising pre-chorus energy: walking shots along the platform, transitional camera movement, slightly warmer highlights against the otherwise cool palette.

# M06 — MIRO

**Performance identity:** Bridge

### Proposed immutable visual identity

- Slightly oblong face with a tapered jaw and subtle natural asymmetry.
- Medium brows, with the left brow naturally sitting a little higher than the right.
- Expressive almond eyes that can read playful, vulnerable, or intense depending on expression.
- Straight-to-slightly tapered nose.
- Defined cupid's bow and medium lips.
- Medium warm-neutral complexion.
- Wiry lean build with flexible, fluid posture rather than rigid symmetry.

### Visual presence

Adaptability. MIRO should be the member whose expression changes the most while his face remains unmistakable. The mild asymmetry helps avoid synthetic perfection and supports realism.

### Baseline master-reference styling

Dark hair with slightly irregular layered texture, medium-short length. Minimal dewy-neutral makeup. Relaxed dark overshirt or soft structured jacket.

### Last Train Home compatibility

MIRO works in transitional visual moments: moving between platform zones, passing lights, handheld camera movement, and frames that connect the rap section back into melody.

---

# M07 — EON

**Performance identity:** Flare

### Proposed immutable visual identity

- Heart-to-inverted-triangle face with a refined, clearly defined chin rather than an extremely narrow V-line.
- Fine but defined brows with clean spacing.
- Large clear almond eyes with a higher visible upper-lid line than the rest of the group.
- Narrow straight nose with elegant proportions.
- Medium-full lips with a clean, expressive mouth shape.
- Light neutral-cool complexion.
- Tall-looking slim frame, long neck, long limb proportions, visually light rather than physically broad.

### Visual presence

Height and brightness in the composition. EON should feel visually "higher" and more luminous when he enters a climax without being styled as ethereal in every era.

### Baseline master-reference styling

Near-black hair with clean layered length and enough forehead exposure to preserve face shape. Minimal luminous makeup. Simple black mock-neck or narrow-collar structured layer.

### Last Train Home compatibility

EON should be held back visually just as his high register is held back musically: stronger presence later in the MV, overhead platform lighting, upper-frame compositions, and brighter rim light during the final chorus.

## Group-level silhouette test

When placed side by side with similar neutral hair and clothing, the lineup should still read distinctly:

- **VALE:** long grounded silhouette, heavier brow/eyes, broad shoulder line.
- **SOREN:** broader face and stronger athletic mass.
- **NOEN:** soft oval face and slender intimate presence.
- **ROOK:** angular diamond face and compact kinetic build.
- **ELIAN:** balanced open face and bright eye expression.
- **MIRO:** oblong asymmetric face and fluid wiry posture.
- **EON:** refined heart-shaped face and long slim proportions.

If generated results make two members look too similar, reject the weaker result rather than canonizing both.

## Debut-era baseline — Last Train Home

The approved debut should not permanently define UNFRAME's style, but it should provide the first visual compatibility test.

Recommended debut baseline:

- predominantly natural dark hair;
- charcoal, black, navy, stone, and muted steel wardrobe;
- contemporary minimal tailoring, knitwear, structured coats, subtle texture differences;
- realistic late-night station lighting;
- cool desaturated image with selective warm practical lights;
- natural skin texture;
- restrained accessories and no loud visible branding;
- each member styled individually enough to remain recognizable without breaking group cohesion.

## Approval rule

The user may approve the full visual architecture, request revisions to specific members, or reject the visual direction. Do not mark facial/body traits canonical and do not create final master-reference sheets until explicit approval is received.
