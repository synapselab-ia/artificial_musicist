# GROUP BIBLE

Status: **IN DEVELOPMENT**

## Project premise

Build a coherent fictional seven-member male K-pop group whose releases, members, visual identities, eras, and discography remain internally consistent across separate Flow Music projects and future ChatGPT sessions.

## Core design principles

### 1. Persistent characters

The seven members must become stable fictional people rather than disposable prompt roles. Once approved, each member receives a permanent member ID, stage name, visual identity, vocal/rap identity, performance personality, and recurring strengths.

### 2. Genre flexibility

The group may release cinematic R&B-pop, hip-hop, pop-rock, electronic music, alternative pop, synth-pop, or other compatible styles. Genre changes should serve each concept rather than being random.

### 3. Strong member contrast

Songs should exploit differences between low vocals, intimate vocals, power vocals, high vocals, deep rap, technical rap, and melodic rap rather than treating the group as one generic male voice.

### 4. Evolution across eras

Hair, makeup, wardrobe, palette, and visual direction may change by era while facial identity and member recognizability remain stable.

### 5. Originality

The group may inherit broad qualities associated with top-tier K-pop—strong hooks, vocal/rap contrast, ambitious production, dramatic progression, layered group vocals, cinematic presentation—but must not imitate a real group's songs or members.

## Founding release

**Debut:** `Last Train Home`

The approved debut establishes the project's first quality benchmark and proves that an emotionally cinematic release can work for the group.
