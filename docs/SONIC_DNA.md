# SONIC DNA

Status: **FOUNDING PRINCIPLES**

## Group-level DNA

The group should not be locked to one genre. Its recurring musical identity is defined by the following principles:

- modern male K-pop production with ambitious arrangement;
- memorable, concise hooks;
- clear contrast between individual vocalists and rappers;
- songs that evolve materially from beginning to end;
- pre-choruses that earn their choruses;
- choruses with a real payoff rather than automatic anti-drops;
- purposeful bridges;
- final choruses that develop rather than simply copy Chorus 1;
- layered group vocals used with intention;
- strong low-end and drum design when the concept calls for power;
- enough negative space in early sections to preserve headroom for later escalation;
- coherent symbolic/lyrical worlds instead of unrelated metaphor stacking.

## Production lesson from rejected Main Character generation

The user rejected the generated `Main Character` result because the instrumental felt too weak and the voices lacked the desired power.

Therefore, when a concept is intended to be high-impact:

- specify drum size, low-end weight, and midrange instrumental density concretely;
- give lead singers explicit opportunities for strong chest-dominant delivery and belts;
- create meaningful dynamic contrast before high-power sections;
- do not assume upbeat groove alone will produce sufficient impact.

This is a production lesson, not a rule that every release must be heavy.
